Figure to sprite

A picture turns to type, and the type turns to a 1-bit sprite that plays on in the rain. Three ways across and four palettes, cut from a hero that kept one of each.

The figure and the sprite are generated in code: a lit 3D bust and a sailboat on a 256 by 256 grid, 12 frames. Swap placeholders.js for your own picture and film and the transitions follow.

Notes

The loop is 12 seconds: the picture, a sweep to type, a hold, the transition, the sprite. The rain falls from the first frame to the last. Frames run only while the stage is on screen and the tab is showing. Press play, or drag the scrubber to stop on any frame.
