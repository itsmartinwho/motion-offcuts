Floating marks

A few marks cross a hero picture while a figure stands in it. Five ways to do it, from a WebGL sphere to a quiet index. Each one is a module you can lift.

The picture here is generated in code: a satin backdrop, a bust, three invented marks. Swap placeholders.js for your own and the pieces follow.

Notes

Each piece loops. Frames run only while the stage is on screen and the tab is showing. Under reduced motion the pieces rest on one frame until you press play. The scrubber under each stage moves the loop by hand.
